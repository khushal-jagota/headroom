# Panels visual basis — charter

The agreed foundation for the Panels UI. Not a mockup spec; the vibe every mockup is judged against.

## The feeling

Ancient mass, modern light. The power of Greek and Roman work — weight, order, proportion —
alluded to, never depicted, inside software that is unmistakably contemporary. A human,
imperfect layer (grain) keeps it warm. The tension between these is the point: regal *and*
modern at once, the way a halftone cobalt bust on a dark card is both.

Power here is not emptiness and not stillness. It is opposing forces held in strict
composition — massive against fine, rough against precise, warm against cold, dense against
open — where everything present is necessary. Clutter is the enemy; so is costume.

## Color system

Three colors form the basis:

- **Warm dark charcoal** — the ground. The world's one material, brown-tinged, never pure
  black. Depth comes from carving it into surface levels, not from voids.
- **Dark blue** — the surface accent. A second material, used liberally where weight is
  needed: anchoring bands, fields behind figures, the surface of what matters most.
- **Matte slate denim** — the accent. Light, faded, desaturated. It never glows; "lit"
  means lifted to denim, not neon.

Type sits in warm bone off-white and muted warm ash. A red-to-green semantic range exists
but appears only where meaning genuinely demands it, tuned mineral and muted to live in
this world.

Aliveness comes from the tonal range — deep recess to bright bone to denim light — like
light on stone. Never from glows, gradients, or saturation.

## Typography

- **Monumental voice:** a modern editorial serif, used only at inscription scale for the
  few big words a page owns. Aggressive scale — violent jumps, no timid middle.
- **Working voice:** a quiet, precise sans for rows, body, metadata.
- **Bridge:** small widely-tracked uppercase labels.

The ancient/modern tension lives inside this pairing on every screen.

## Composition

- **A page has one focus. Let that focus be the focus.** If it is a table, the table gets
  the monumental treatment; everything else recedes into the stone.
- Strict rhythm and alignment — order is the frame that lets opposing forces read as
  composed rather than messy.
- Extremes of weight: slab rules and hairlines; skip the middle.
- No component-library reflexes: no three-pane fills, filter stacks, stat strips, widgets,
  or features we don't have. Every region answers a live question or goes.

## Texture and figures

- Subtle film grain on surfaces everywhere — the human hand.
- Classical figures appear as **backdrops in the stone**: ghosted, stippled, bleeding off
  the edge, behind real content. Underused — at most one per major surface, many surfaces
  none. Never framed as a portrait, never a logo, no laurels, no Latin, no columns.

## What kills it

Literal Roman props. Saturated electric blue. Glow effects and glassy cards. Even-weight
mush. Filled zones. Anything present without a reason.
