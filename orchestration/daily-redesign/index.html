Same screens, a new voice.

The functions and shapes of the screens are locked. What changes is the UI: everything the system says is set in serif; everything that is a control stays sans. Amber is the one accent, spinners mean working, depth belongs only to the ask. All navs are wired — click through the whole app from any page.

Review
One centered decision: skip/open top-right, recap, the ask, the send-back path. Toggle the empty state bottom-right.
Workspace
Roster left, chief of staff at rest on the right — the chief speaks in serif, the rows stay instruments.
Ticket
The document: serif title and prose, one facts line, the leash sentence, recap then the spine of dropdowns (user note, stages with their notes), the ask, the employee alongside.
Day
The morning read: date, serif focus line, brief take, amber watchout, green if-today-lands. Flat, no boxes.
Sprint
Name, meta line (dates · day · done count), the bet, then the items grouped by project — status word and done-fraction on each row, tickets inside. Documents live on their own page, linked top-right.
Sprint documents
Kickoff, mid-sprint review, and sprint review — the sprint's written record, off the tracking page.
Backlog
The shelf: new-item form (title, project/priority segments, deadline, description), then priority groups of rows.
Ideas
The pile: always-open capture with ↩ hint, then the captured list — detail rows expand, serif bodies, relative dates.
