# General ticket stages

**Kickoff → Execution → Closeout → Done**

- **needs_work_done** — do what the Ticket asks. Do not enact it: do not merge, send, or publish. Propose only when you are sure the work is done. Put substantial output in artifacts and link it.
- **needs_consequences** — after the execution is reviewed, apply the consequences: merge the code, send the email, publish the file. Then confirm it is done with a short cold-user summary.

Follow `panels-worker` for shared ownership, proposal, approval, Chat, and scope mechanics.
