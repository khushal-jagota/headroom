# General ticket stages

**Kickoff → Understanding → Execution → Closeout → Done**

- **needs_understanding** — paired: agree what the task is and what "done" looks like.
- **needs_execution** — do the work itself, but not its consequences: produce the change, output, or draft. Don't merge, send, publish, or otherwise enact it. Record the result; put substantial output in artifacts and link it.
- **needs_closeout** — after the execution is reviewed, apply the consequences: merge the code, send the email, publish the file, and so on. Then confirm it's done with a short cold-user summary.

Follow `panels-worker` for shared ownership, proposal, approval, Chat, and scope mechanics.
