# Sprint Kickoff

Date range: 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-12

## Limiting Factor

Vylo needs a more durable product core: people, context, timing, and onboarding need to compound into credible relationship intelligence rather than isolated analysis moments.

## Primary Bet

If Vylo makes people durable — compounding personas/profiles, better entity assignment, and richer context over time — then real-life analysis, onboarding, notifications, and calendar-aware help become meaningfully more useful instead of staying as disconnected feature blobs.

## Supports

- Durable personas / CRM-like people profiles that compound over time.
- Public/product readiness: a small product-accurate landing page with waitlist, simple onboarding first, and PWA/notification reliability.
- Design coherence: usability pass plus a design-philosophy writeup.
- Calendar-aware context shaped as the next major integration without letting it steal the sprint from personas.

## Pre-mortem

- Durable personas becomes an abstract architecture swamp instead of one real usable people/profile slice.
- Landing, onboarding, PWA, and notifications eat the sprint while the core personas campaign never gets deep thought.
- Calendar starts too early and forces bad model decisions before durable personas are clear.
- Onboarding waits for the magical version instead of shipping the simple version that captures the right information.
- The 600-commit `main` push stays blocked because public/product readiness never gets made good-enough.
