Type and stage in Workspace rows

Three practical ways to make ticket type and current workflow stage separately readable without changing Workspace grouping, filtering, ordering, selection, or navigation. Click a row to preview selection; “Hide done” works in every variant.

Stacked byline

The title stays primary. Type and stage become a calm second line, with the existing dot remaining a separate status signal.

  • Strong at: immediate comprehension and long labels.
  • Trade-off: rows become slightly taller.
  • Character: quiet, readable, closest to the ticket detail hierarchy.

Type anchor

A compact, stable leading marker makes worker type highly scannable. The current stage remains written beneath the title.

  • Strong at: comparing mixed ticket types down a long list.
  • Trade-off: introduces a stronger visual device than the current rail.
  • Character: structured and tool-like.

Right metadata rail

Rows keep their current height. Type and stage align as a compact scan column before the existing status dot.

  • Strong at: density and vertical comparison of stages.
  • Trade-off: gives long ticket titles less width.
  • Character: efficient and roster-like.
